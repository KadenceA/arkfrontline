Privacy Policy.
Plain and simple.

This page explains what personal data ARK Frontline collects through this website, why we collect it, how we protect it, and the rights you have over it.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

1. Who we are

ARK Frontline is an inbound customer support outsourcing company based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. We provide dedicated support agents to growing businesses across property, software, hospitality and e-commerce.

For the purposes of this policy, ARK Frontline is the data controller for personal data collected through this website. You can reach us at hello@arkfrontline.com or +18154964331.

2. What data we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us. Through the Request a Free Audit form on our contact page, we collect:

  • Your full name
  • Your business email address
  • Your company name
  • Your industry (selected from a list)
  • Any optional details you choose to include about your needs (channels, volume, hours, etc.)

If you contact us directly by email or phone, we also receive whatever information you choose to share in that message.

Data we do not collect

We do not run advertising trackers, we do not use cookies for profiling, and we do not collect payment or financial information through this website. At the time of writing, the site does not use analytics tools that identify individual visitors.

3. How we use your data

We use the data you submit for these purposes only:

  • To respond to your audit request and arrange a call
  • To understand your support needs so we can prepare a relevant proposal
  • To communicate with you about your inquiry, and — if we enter a client relationship — to deliver the service
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations if a contract is signed

We do not sell your data, rent it, or share it with advertisers. We do not use it for automated profiling or decision-making.

4. Legal basis for processing

Under Ethiopia's Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024, we process your data on the basis of your consent (when you submit the form) and our legitimate interest in responding to business inquiries. Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, we rely on your consent and, where relevant, our legitimate interest in responding to a business-to-business inquiry. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us.

5. How long we keep your data

If you submit an inquiry and we do not enter a client relationship, we keep your data for up to 24 months from our last contact, so we can pick up the conversation if you return. If we become your support provider, we retain the data for the duration of the contract and for as long as required by Ethiopian accounting and tax law afterwards. You can ask us to delete your data sooner at any time — see Section 9.

6. Who we share data with

We keep sharing to a minimum. We may share your data with:

  • Our email and hosting providers — the services that deliver our website and receive form submissions. These providers process data on our instructions only.
  • Professional advisers (e.g. accountants or lawyers) — only if required to deliver the service or meet a legal obligation.
  • Authorities — only where we are legally required to disclose.

We do not sell or trade personal data.

7. International transfers

ARK Frontline is based in Ethiopia. Some of the services we use to run this website — for example Google Fonts (for typography) and our Cloudflare hosting — may process technical data (such as your IP address) outside Ethiopia, including in the EU and the United States. Where this happens, we rely on the providers' standard contractual safeguards and keep the data limited to what is needed to deliver the website.

8. How we protect your data

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the data you send us — including limiting access to the inbox that receives form submissions, using reputable hosting and email providers, and keeping our software up to date. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we work to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct any inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete your data (the "right to be forgotten")
  • Object to or restrict how we process it
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@arkfrontline.com with the subject line "Privacy request". We will respond within 30 days, and may ask you to verify your identity before we act.

10. Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The only third-party requests are the Google Fonts stylesheet used for typography, which may receive your IP address as part of normal web delivery. If we add analytics or other cookies in future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

11. Children's privacy

This website is intended for business use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has submitted data to us, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or the law. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be highlighted on the site where practical. We encourage you to review this page occasionally.
