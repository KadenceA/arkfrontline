We started ARK Frontline after watching too many growing businesses lose customers to slow replies. The pattern was always the same: a small team doing great work, an inbox that outgrew them, and no affordable way to add trained hands without hiring, onboarding and managing a whole new department.

So we built the team we wished existed — dedicated agents, trained on your product before day one, working inside your tools, measured on the numbers that actually matter to you. Based in Ethiopia, built for time zones that need coverage while your team sleeps.

Dedicated Team

Scale On Demand

How We Hire
and Train.

Talk to Us
